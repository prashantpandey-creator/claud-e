# Commercial licence

`meditate` is released under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**
(see LICENSE). That licence already covers a lot of people, free:

- yourself, on your own projects, hobby work, study, experiments
- charities, schools, universities, public research, government

**If you use it as part of your paid work, you need a commercial licence.**
That includes using it at a company, on client work, or inside a product you
sell — regardless of whether you paid for it or built on it.

## What a commercial licence grants

- use by you and your company for any purpose, including commercial
- no per-seat reporting, no phone-home, no licence key in the software
- the same source you can already read; nothing is withheld from the free tier

## What it does not grant

- reselling `meditate`, or offering it as a hosted or managed service
- removing the licence notices
